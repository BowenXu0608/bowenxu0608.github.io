# Polar Acts I-II gameplay

## Each hut's walk path kept, November 12, 2026 (0.6.149-test)

This is the fix the measurement after 0.6.148-test found ([results](../logs/evidence/2026-11-12/walk-path-kept/results.md)). No rule, save, route or drawn pixel changes.

- **Kept walk paths.** `PolarWorldController.WalkPath(sector, hut)` builds a hut's walk path once and keeps it in `walkPaths`, beside `siteFrames` and under the same key.
  - The path is the catalog's `hutWalkPath` turned by the hut's frame, with the outside points put on the ground (`GroundAt`) and everything raised 3 cm.
  - Both caches are cleared where `Datum` is set.
  - `PolarMvpController.SiteWalkPath(site)` returns the kept array. Its callers (`InsideHut`, `WalkOut`, `LocalEntryPath`, `Collect`, the acceptance walk and the Editor captures) read or copy it. A new caller must not change it.
  - Main thread only.
- **Measured.** In a walk day:
  - a route arriving (`Collect`) takes a median 0.05 ms, where it took 0.82;
  - the step's slowest frame is 3.14 ms in minutes 10 to 15, where it was 3.78.

  The slowest frames left are long walks: terrain, support and walking-effort tests.
- **Markers.** `Polar.Hut.WalkPath` times a path being built.
- **The line cast near the player** (measured afterwards, [results](../logs/evidence/2026-11-12/line-cast-near/results.md)).
  - `-polarSaveRateNear` (`polar_save_rate.py --walk --near`, `PolarSaveRateRun.Near`) stands the player 6 m out from one explorer's hut door before the timing starts, so that explorer's legs run `Obstructed`.
  - Its 912 casts in five minutes took a median 0.001 ms each and at most 0.13 ms, about 1% of a step near the player.
  - None stopped a leg.

## A lighter explorers' step, November 12, 2026 (0.6.148-test)

This is the explorers' step peak left by 0.6.147-test ([results](../logs/evidence/2026-11-12/save-step-peaks/results.md)). No rule, save, route or drawn pixel changes.

- **Routes on the ground off the main thread.** A route search's task (`Task.Run` in `PolarNpcSimulation`) straightens the cells, then puts each on the ground (`Ground`: `CellPosition`, then `GroundAt`) on the same worker. `Collect` only adds the points. `GroundAt` reads the world's data and the thread-local height memos, and touches no Unity object. A cell with no ground now ends the search as "Route unavailable"; before, it threw in `Collect`.
- **Each hut's place and turn is kept.** `PolarWorldController.SiteFrame(sector, hut)` keeps the hut's origin and turn. It is cleared where `Datum` is set, so it is worked out again for a new world. `PolarMvpController.SiteWorldPoint`, `SetExposure` and `ClearLine` read it, so the explorers' support, exposure and line-of-sight tests no longer work it out at each call. Main thread only.
- **Boulders without allocation.** `PolarBoulderField.Gather` fills one kept list with `Within`'s boulders in `Within`'s order, for `Blocking` and `Outside`. Neither calls itself while the list is read. An instance is used by one thread; `Tools/PolarWorldCheck` makes one per thread. The leg test (`legClear`), the cell test (`walkCells`) and `PolarWorldController.CanMoveTerrain`'s walkable test (`moveCells`) are kept delegates.
- **Plain loops.** Contact and a rifle in sight (`RifleInSight`) are plain loops. Waypoints round a boulder are inserted one by one.
- **What the rare 4 ms steps spend** (measured after the package, [results](../logs/evidence/2026-11-12/step-unmarked/results.md)). A route arriving (`Collect`) builds two huts' walk paths (`SiteWalkPath`), 0.4 to 1.4 ms each, mostly the 35 outside points' `GroundAt`. That and the explorer's first walk on the route (1.2 to 1.7 ms) make 14 of a walk day's 16 slowest step frames. The leg's line cast (`Obstructed`) runs only within 180 m of the player and did not run in the walk day. A hut's walk path depends only on the hut and `Datum`, so it is now kept as `SiteFrame` is (0.6.149-test, above).
- **Gestures.** `PolarPhysicalGesture.Play` gives an explorer's gesture to `PolarNpcSimulation.Gesture`, which finds the explorer's view by id among the views shown. Before, it searched every `PolarCharacterPresentation` in the scene for the actor's marker.
- **Also.**
  - `PolarItemDefinition.Of` reads an array indexed by kind.
  - `PolarGeometry.Project` takes each wave's sine once. The results are the same bit for bit, and twenty seeds give the same summaries.
- **Measured.** In a free walk day the step's slowest frame went from 6.13 to 4.18 ms (minutes 2 to 6) and from 8.46 to 3.83 ms (minutes 10 to 15). Frames over 4 ms went from 52 to 2.

## Door gestures find the door in a list, November 12, 2026 (0.6.147-test)

This is the door-check cost left by 0.6.145-test ([results](../logs/evidence/2026-11-12/door-gesture-lookup/results.md)). No rule, save or drawn pixel changes.

- **`PolarPhysicalGesture.Target`** reaches a door's handle on the door from `PolarHutDoor.Of(site)`. That door comes from the list of enabled doors (`OnEnable`/`OnDisable`) that `PolarHutDoor.Solid` uses for door passages since 0.6.137-test. The hut's own door with collision comes before a far hut's door without (`PolarWorldStreamer`'s proxies), which is the rule the old walk of `game.World`'s hierarchy had. That walk took 2 to 6 ms at every door an explorer opened or closed.
- **A hut's dressing.** Its fire's flare on light and refuel (`Play`) and its heater (`Target`) come from `PolarPhysicalGesture.Dressing(site)`: the `PolarArtHutDressing` on that door's own GameObject. `PolarSiteView` puts a hut's door and dressing on the site view. Far huts have no dressing.
- **A new door or dressing** must stay on the site view, or this lookup misses it.

## Explorers step at their own moments, November 12, 2026 (0.6.145-test)

The explorers' once-a-second step, an item the user left open ([results](../logs/evidence/2026-11-12/explorer-step-spread/results.md)). No rule, save or drawn pixel changes.

- **The schedule.** `PolarSurvivalSimulation.Step` calls `StepNpcs(before, dt)` at every rules step, with the NPC clock (`npcAccumulator`) before the step and the step's length. A person's moment is `Offset(rules, id)`, which is id modulo `StepSlots` (20) twentieths of `npcStep`. `Due(rules, id, before, dt)` says whether that moment falls in the rules step, with the clock's own 1e-7 guard. `PolarNpcSimulation.Step(before, frame)` steps only those who are due, each by one full `npcStep`, in id order. So each person still steps once a game second by one second, but at 60 fps no rules step holds more than one person; at 10x sleep it is two. The player in autopilot (id 0) steps at the whole second, where everyone stepped before. `Step(dt)` still steps everyone at once, for the Editor capture tools.
- **Since a person's step.** `Since(state, id)` gives the game seconds since a person's own last moment, from 0 up to one `npcStep`. It replaces the clock's remainder wherever that stood for "since the step":
  - the explorer view walks each step from `seconds - Since`;
  - `SetExposure` takes explorers' wind heading at `-Since`;
  - `WarmShelter` works it out ahead at `npcStep - Since`. That is the moment the person steps, so its cache still matches exactly.
- **Saves and order.** The clock is the same saved field, and the moments come from the ids, so a save made before this change continues on the new schedule. Explorers now step in turn through the second, not all in one frame. Each step still sees the others where their own last step left them. What one explorer does, for example taking a unit, is seen by the next one to step, as before.
- **The walk capture** (`PolarFiveAreasCapture.Walk.cs`) stages the clock one frame before its explorer's moment, so the walk starts at frame 0.

## Explorers' shelter through a wind turn, and night fall measured again, November 12, 2026 (0.6.141-test)

The user's "Yes, fix it here" on the explorers' night-fall frames ([results](../logs/evidence/2026-11-12/night-fall-frames/results.md); the director's part is in [cloud NPCs](polar-cloud-npcs.md)). No save or drawn pixel changes. The player's shelter and wind are computed as before.

- **The problem.** The wind turns over 20 s whenever its four-hour direction changes. For seed 777 that is every four game hours from 10:00, 22:00 among them. Since 0.6.137-test, `WarmShelter` works out explorers' terrain shelter two explorers a frame between rules steps, and the step's `SetExposure` uses that value if it is within 0.01 degree of the heading. During a turn the heading had moved on by the step, so nothing matched, and the step's frame worked every explorer out again: 0.3 ms each, 4 to 13 ms a step.
- **The fix.**
  - `PolarWeatherTransition.WindHeading(s, ahead)` gives the heading `ahead` seconds from now, or earlier if negative, along a turn under way. A finished turn keeps its end heading. Called without `ahead`, it gives every caller what it gave before.
  - Explorers' shelter takes the heading at the rules step's whole second: `SetExposure` passes `-npcAccumulator` for explorers and uses the moment's heading for the player. `WarmShelter` works it out one step ahead (`npcStep - npcAccumulator`). Wind time and the step's counter advance by the same amount in every fixed step, so the two agree exactly.
- **What explorers feel.** It changes only inside a turn, by a fixed step's overshoot: at 60 fps at most about 0.2 degrees of heading. It no longer depends on the frame rate. Outside turns every value is as before.
- **Measured** in a free walk day (`Tools/polar_save_rate.py --walk --profile`):
  - slow frames with 3 ms or more of `Polar.Npc.Senses`: 45 to 0;
  - slow frames inside the turns: 47 to 23;
  - the game's own frames over 25 ms: 198 to 179.
- **Night fall, read again.** 0.6.140-test said the night requests made "about five frames of 27 to 54 ms". That was wrong:
  - the request bunch made one frame of about 19 ms without call stacks, now spread by one start a frame;
  - 22:00's wind turn added 8.5 ms of shelter work to the first night step, fixed above;
  - the other night frames were code compiled on first use (the first sleep, the first harpoon on the ground), a worker-thread collection after route searches, and a save, each on a busy machine.

## When the game saves, November 10, 2026 (0.6.135-test)

The user's choice after the live save-rate measurement ([design decisions](../../design-decisions.md), [results](../logs/evidence/2026-11-10/save-cadence/results.md)). The save file's contents and the rules of play are unchanged.

- **Autosave** (`PolarMvpController.Autosave`): a change of the state (`PolarGameplayState.Changed`) is saved in the background once the last save is `AutosaveGap` = 5 real seconds old (it was half a second); the 60 s autosave (`rules.autosaveSeconds` of play) is as it was; a failed save is tried again after `RetryGap` = half a second. Leaving, quitting, the expedition's end and `SaveNow` save at once.
- **A hut seen again** (`PolarActions.ObserveExterior`, every person and every hut in sight each 5 game seconds): when nothing but the record's times moves, the state is not marked changed, and the times go out with the next save. A first sight, or a different entrance, structure, habitable or stove state, or a record that had come from someone else, calls `Changed` as before.
- **Only the autosave reads the state's `revision`**, so nothing else follows the stamp.

## The ice holds to its edge; the dip, November 3, 2026 (0.6.100-test)

M2 of the deep-water plan:
- **One hole line.** `PolarThinIce.Hole(pond, plate)` (pure, `PolarThinIceData.cs`) is the visible edge of a broken plate's hole: the plate opened by a 0.5-1.2 m radius and its line pushed in by fractal noise, as the art drew it. The art's rim strips, the rim colliders (`PolarThinIceRuntime.Rims`), the rules (`SupportAt`) and climbing out (`ClimbOut`) all use it. Each rim strip belongs to the unbroken neighbour across its edge; it tapers out within 0.6 m of an edge shared with another hole, so merged holes open as one. Standing on a strip loads its neighbour.
- **Explorers.** `PolarMvpController.StepEnvironment` asks `PolarThinIce.SupportAt(pond, x, z)` instead of the plate polygon: water inside a hole's line, the neighbour's plate on a rim. They swim to the line and climb onto the ice 0.4 m past it, so the body hangs at the line (`ClimbOut`).
- **The player's width.** Over a broken plate or in a pond's water the player's capsule is 0.15 m wide instead of 0.35 (`PolarSurveyPlayer.Narrow`), so the player stands on a rim up to its visible edge, goes in past it and swims up to it.

## Hard walking, October 30, 2026 (0.6.87-test)

The land and the weather make walking hard, for the player and every explorer alike. This is the user's decision in `docs/design-decisions.md` (top section), replacing October 17's "steep ground does not slow them". The pure rules are in `PolarWalkEffort` (no Unity; checked by `Tools/PolarGameplayCheck/WalkEffortChecks.cs`, 20 checks).
- **Speed.** `PolarMvpController.Effort(actor, at, direction)` multiplies three factors, never below `Floor` .3, on top of the cell's travel cost:
  - `Slope(World.Rise(at, direction))`. `Rise` is the height difference from .25 m behind to .75 m ahead, and is 0 over a gap or water. It replaces the unused `SlopeSpeed`/`PlayerSlopeSpeed`.
  - `Wind(Strength(wind, gust, terrainShelter, ShelterFactor), dot(direction, Downwind()))`. `Downwind` is (cos h, 0, sin h) of `PolarWeatherTransition.WindHeading`.
  - `Drift(Streamer.SnowAt(at))`.

  The player's `PolarSurveyPlayer.Move` multiplies its speed by `GameplayEffort` while walking in gameplay and keeps the result as `LastEffort`. Explorers' `Move` and `Walk` multiply by the same `Effort`.
- **Gust shove.** `PolarSurveyPlayer.Shove` = `Downwind() * PolarWalkEffort.Shove(Gust, Wind(player))` is set each frame and added to a walking player's motion. Explorers are not shoved.
- **Footing.** `PolarMvpController.Footing` runs once a game second for the player (walking) and after each explorer step. `Blown` (gust ≥ .92, wind strength ≥ .85, 6% a second, a minute apart) calls `PolarActions.KnockDown`. `Slips` (on `PolarSurfaceKind.Ice`, running or rise under -.268, 15% a second, 20 s apart) falls with "Slipped on the ice". Rolls hash the seed, the person and the floored second.
- **Drops.** `PolarSurveyPlayer` keeps the top of a fall (`fallTop`, reset on leaving water) and raises `Landed(drop)` on Falling to Walking, except while streaming holds the body. `PolarMvpController.Landed` records `LandedAt`/`LandedDrop` from `Grunt` 1.2 m. From `Down` 3.5 m it calls `Actions.Fall(player, FallDamage(drop, PolarSurface.Give(Under)), "Fell N m")`. Explorers' routes skirt drops (`PolarNpcSimulation.Skirt`), so they fall only from gusts and slips.
- **The fall rule.** `PolarActions.Fall(actor, damage, why)` does nothing to the dead or the already down. Otherwise it:
  - cancels the action;
  - sets `fallenAt`/`fallSeconds` (`DownSeconds`: 1 s going, `Lie` 1.5-4 s, 2.75 s rising);
  - stops moving;
  - remembers the cause;
  - and loses health as "Fall".

  `lastDamageAt` is untouched, so a fall ends no talk, alarms nobody and is no attack. `Down(actor)` is true until `fallenAt + fallSeconds`. While the player is down, movement and input stop. The save keeps both fields, which `PolarSaveService` validates (finite, not in the future, 0-10 s).
- **Explorers.** A down explorer does nothing that step. On its first step down it is told `Fell(actor)`: "A gust knocked you down" / "You slipped on the ice and fell" / "You fell", then its health and "You will be up in a few seconds". `PolarNpcManual` has a "Footing." paragraph, and `WalkHours` divides by `Snow` .85 (open snow's usual effort).
- **Presentation.** No new clips.
  - `PolarNpcView` plays "Death" for a drop or a slip (from .3 s) or "DeathShot" for a gust (from 1.2 s, thrown back). It holds the body on the floor at the fall's yaw, then plays "UpFast" for the last 2.75 s. Hits and talk gestures wait.
  - `PolarPlayerBodyView.Rest` plays the same clips at the player's place and puts the body back when the fall ends.
  - The view is in `rendering-streaming.md`; the sounds are in `polar-audio.md`.

## Body reflexes, executor steps and truer failure texts: October 29, 2026 (0.6.81-test)

Part of [Claude explorers](polar-cloud-npcs.md#claude-explorers-who-know-the-game-and-talk-in-turn-october-29-2026-0681-test) ([decisions](../../design-decisions.md#claude-explorers-shared-training-and-body-reflexes-october-29-2026-0681-test)).
- **Reflexes** (`PolarNpcSimulation.Reflex`, every tick before `Execute`; never during aim, shoot, stab or throw).
  - **Eat.** Satiety under `hungerDanger` eats one unit of canned food, a prepared ration, or a frozen ration at a lit fire. The current command is kept.
  - **Warmth.** The first time temperature falls under `freezingTemperature` in a cold spell, unless the explorer is at a lit fire or its current command already makes for warmth. The spell ends at `CautiousTemperature` and is unsaved. The reflex replaces the plan with:
    - pitch, when there is no tent within 12 m and no known hut within 150 m;
    - tent, then burner on or filled;
    - or travel, shelter, then stove lit or filled.

    It does nothing when the explorer is already near its shelter.
  - Each reflex is recorded as an event ("Ate by reflex", "Sought warmth by reflex").
  - `EatReflexes` and `WarmthReflexes` count reflexes for the day reports.
- **Warnings.** Health drained by hunger or cold warns again for every 10 hp lost.
- **Executor steps.**
  - `strike` from inside the explorer's own tent leaves by the entrance first.
  - `sleep` enters a tent it may sleep in within 12 m, unless a hut is here and the tent is not entered.
  - `watch` walks there first.
  - `TentFor` resolves an id that is no longer a pitched tent to the nearest tent the explorer owns, or may sleep in, within 60 m.
  - A tent that is gone fails "That tent is no longer pitched".
  - `EnterTent` fails the step with "I cannot reach the tent" when its walk is blocked, or with the flap's own reason when the flap won't open from where the explorer stands. An explorer already inside counts as arrived. In `nk-live-01`, Moss stood 3.6 m above its own tent, pitched at the foot of a drop, for nine game minutes until dying of cold, and chose the same plan 17 times.
  - `take` and `pour` use a unit of the named kind (for pour, a flask with oil) that `CanReachPhysical` reaches, before one behind others. A box shows each kind by one unit's id, and in the first three-day run (`nk-live-01`) 923 of 1,188 takes failed on units at the back. The pour help now names the box's oil flask (it had said "fuel can").
  - **Reach in huts away from the player** (`PolarMvpController.CanReachUnloaded`). The test for an item left the item's own open box in as a blocker. A box drawn with a mesh collider stands there as that mesh's whole bounds, so nothing inside could be reached: in `nk-live-02`, 80 of 128 takes failed, some from crates holding only tins. That box's body is now left out (`UnloadedPhysicalBlocks(..., holder)`); its lid and the other items still block, so items behind others stay out of reach. Loaded huts and the player are unchanged.
- **Failure texts** (`PolarActions`, `PolarActions.Physical`, for players and explorers):
  - Sleeping or keeping watch under `RestTemperature` fails "Too cold to sleep: warm up first" or "Too cold to keep watch: warm up first". For watch, the check comes after the place checks.
  - Striking from inside fails "Step out of the tent to strike it" (before the occupancy check).
  - `ContainerFailure` gives taking and putting back one message for each cause.
  - Captures: `PolarFailureTextCapture` (Before/After, `Logs/ProjectUpdateCapture/failure-texts`).
- **Checks.** `Tools/PolarGameplayCheck` 2704: the striker inside, cold watch, and the four container causes.

## Rifle shot and item placement past the player's own body: October 18, 2026 (0.6.29-test)

0.6.28-test made E on a person skip the player's own character controller ([cloud NPCs](polar-cloud-npcs.md#e-on-a-person-and-words-to-nobody-october-18-2026-0628-test)). Two more rays from the eye still took the first physics hit: the eye is inside that capsule ('Surveyor', layer 0; the mask leaves out only the ViewModel layer 10), and PhysX sometimes reports it at 0 m. A rifle shot could then silently do nothing, and the placement preview could take the eye as its aim point.
- **One aim.** `PolarMvpController.Aimed(eye, range, out hit)` is the nearest hit along the eye's ray within `range`, past the player's own body. The focus and E (`Aimed(eye)`, within `interactionMetres`), item placement (`UpdatePlacement`, within `interactionMetres`) and the rifle shot (`PolarWeaponView`, within `rifleRange`, 140 m) all use it.
- **Full buffer.** Non-allocating ray hits come in no order, and a full buffer may leave out the nearest one. When the 16-hit buffer fills, it doubles and the ray is cast again, so a long shot across many colliders still finds the nearest.
- **Checks.** The cot probe's rifle check and the placement diagnostic note use the same aim as the game.

## Immediate cooperation terms: October 18, 2026 (0.6.25-test)

Offers between any two people (`PolarActions.Offer`, `AcceptTrade`) carry concrete terms ([NPC agency](polar-cloud-npcs.md#npc-agency-october-18-2026-0625-test)).
- **Terms** (`PolarTradeOffer`): items and counts from each side; kinds and counts asked of the recipient (`asks`) and a number of its records (`askRecords`), which it answers with actual items and records when it accepts; records each side gives, pinned to their observation time; a pitched tent of either side whose guest place goes to the other; and the 24-hour promise. Any carried item except the backpack and winter clothes can be offered, by anyone.
- **Counteroffers.** A new offer between the two replaces one not yet committed, so changed terms clear every acceptance.
- **Commit.** When both have accepted, every term is checked first (both alive and near, stock, pinned records, the tent place, weight), then items, records, the guest place and the promise all commit together. If any check fails nothing changes and both acceptances clear. Accepting again repeats nothing.
- **Tent invitations** are offers of the tent's guest place; only the other's own acceptance makes them the guest.
- Legacy requested offers load as one ask.

## Keepsakes: October 5, 2026 (source only)

Completes item 15 of the Acts I–II list ([plan](../plans/polar-act12-mvp.md)), following GDD 5.9: "Each person carries one personal object with no gameplay power." The MVP has no ship, so by user decision the end screen stands in for the epilogue. No NPC-owned file changed.

- **One each.** `PolarGameplayFactory.Create` gives every person a `Keepsake` last, after the world stock, so every other item keeps its id. `PolarItemInstance.keepsakeOf` records whose it is (-1 on every other item).
- **Physical.** It weighs 0.05 kg and rides in a pocket: `PolarItemDefinition.Stowed` returns Worn for it wherever an item is picked up or handed over, so it needs no backpack and the backpack can still come off. `Equip` refuses it ("A keepsake stays in your pocket").
- **Kinds.** Each person's kind comes from the seed through a stable hash: photograph, letter, pipe, pocket watch, ring, harmonica, locket or medal (`PolarPersonalRecords.KeepsakeKind`). The list is fixed, because changing it would rename keepsakes in saved expeditions.
- **Names.** "Your letter"; "Adler's photograph" once you have met Adler; otherwise "A photograph" (`KeepsakeName`, using the same `Knew` rule as the diary).
- **Moving it.**
  - It can be dropped, picked up, given, or put in an exchange through the existing offer flow.
  - The request lists are unchanged, so nobody can ask for one.
  - Explorers never hand theirs over, because `OfferSafe` lets them give only food and cartridges, but they may accept yours.
  - Explorers do not pick up ground items, so a body keeps its keepsake until the player takes it. A death leaves it at the body like every other possession.
- **Records.**
  - A keepsake handed over is remembered as "Gave keepsake" and "Received keepsake". A gift of keepsakes alone records no "Exchange completed".
  - Picking up someone else's keepsake from the ground records "Found keepsake", with its owner and the nearest hut within 150 m. Picking up one you dropped yourself records nothing.
  - The diary reads "Gave Adler a keepsake.", "Adler gave me a keepsake." and "Found Adler's photograph near District 03, Hut 02." A stranger's reads "Found a photograph.". The roster history adds "You gave them a keepsake" and "They gave you a keepsake".

## Firelight: October 5, 2026 (source only)

Completes item 7 of the Acts I–II list ([plan](../plans/polar-act12-mvp.md)), following GDD 3.4.2: "Firelight is visible at night in clear weather and hidden by blizzards; there is no long-range smoke." By user decision it follows real visibility. Weather rates are unchanged, so no clear weather is added.

- **When a hut shows firelight.** `PolarActions.Lit(site)` is `stoveUsable && stoveLitres > 0`, the same test the hut's own stove light uses. Firelight exists only at night (`State.Night`, 22:00–06:00).
- **How far it carries.** `PolarActions.FirelightRange(state)` is the distance at which the rendered exp² fog of the current weather (`PolarWeatherProfiles.fog`) leaves 2% of the light, capped at 3 km:

  | Weather | Range |
  |---|---|
  | Clear night | 3,000 m (the cap) |
  | Medium snow | 247 m |
  | Heavy snow | 124 m |
  | Snowstorm | 49 m |
  | Blizzard | 23 m, so only at the door |

  Huts stood at least 900 m apart (1,800 m in expeditions started since October 7), so a neighbour's fire shows only on a clear night (about 2% of nights in v6) or when you are already close.
- **Perception, the same for everyone.** `PolarActions.ObserveFirelight(observer)` runs from the world step for every awake, living person at night.
  - **Throttle:** each person looks at most every 5 world seconds.
  - **What counts:** lit huts within range, beyond 15 m, that `CanSeeSite` accepts. It needs two things:
    - **The light's side facing the observer.** Light leaves an intact hut only through its front window (`PolarHutLayout.WindowPoint`, local −Z), so its fire is seen only from that side. A damaged or ruined hut lets light out on every side; there the light comes from the stove itself.
    - **A clear terrain sight line to that point.** The step is coarser at long range, and it works for unloaded terrain.

    Both rules live in `PolarMvpController.FirelightPoint` and `FirelightFaces`.
  - **Once per hut per night, a sighting:**
    - stamps `PolarLocationRecord.firelightSeenAt` (default −1, so older v6 saves load empty; shared copies keep it), creating an exterior record if the hut was unknown;
    - adds a first-hand belief, kind "Firelight": "Firelight at District 03, Hut 07.";
    - records "Saw firelight" for the diary.
  - The belief raises `informationRevision`, so NPC planners see it.
- **On screen.** `PolarFirelightView` places a pooled, camera-facing glow for each lit hut within range. It uses the same light point and facing rule as perception, so the glow and the journal agree: from behind an intact hut there is neither.
  - Its brightness is the night fade (in from 21:00 to 22:00, out from 06:00 to 07:00) × the same exp² fog.
  - It stays about four pixels wide far away, and in falling snow it spreads into a wider, fainter halo (size × (1 + fog × distance)). It is depth-tested so terrain and huts hide it, and is hidden within 15 m, where the interior stove light shows.
  - It is independent of terrain streaming, so distant huts glow too.
  - The shader is `Resources/Polar/PolarFirelightGlow.shader`: additive, with a procedural falloff and no texture.
  - There is no smoke; the chimney is unchanged.
- **Records.**
  - The field journal lists the belief.
  - The diary adds "Saw firelight at …".
  - The field map's selected record adds "Firelight seen: day N, HH:MM".
- **Checks.** `Tools/PolarGameplayCheck` passes 490/490, with 17 new checks, plus the 20,607 v6 checks. The new checks cover:
  - the range per weather;
  - no fire by day, unlit or broken stoves, beyond range, with sight blocked, or within 15 m;
  - once a night, and again the next night;
  - sleepers and the dead;
  - an NPC seeing the same fire;
  - an unknown hut getting a record;

  - the diary;
  - shared records.

## Safeguards, roster record and diary: October 3, 2026 (source only)

Completes items 9, 14 and 16 of the Acts I–II list ([plan](../plans/polar-act12-mvp.md)). The state version stays 5: the new records load empty on existing version-5 saves.

**Visible aim.** While the player holds the rifle raised (right mouse button), `PolarWeaponView` calls `PolarActions.Aim` with the target:
- the living person nearest the crosshair whom the player can see;
- within that person's width plus one degree, and within `rifleRange`.

The aim start is kept in real time, as NPC aiming is. Lowering the rifle, opening a panel, stowing it, sleeping or dying clears it. NPC snapshots already read it ("Aiming a rifle at me"), and NPC watchers already wake sleepers on it.

`PolarActions.ObserveAims` runs every world step and notices anyone's new aim:
- the person aimed at, if awake and able to see the aimer, gets a first-hand "Aimed" belief;
- witnesses who see both people get one through `PolarNpcInformation.Witness`;
- a 10-game-second window per pair stops repeats.

Firing stays immediate (user decision). The player's shot has no warning delay, and NPC `TryShot` is unchanged. A shot that hits a dead body no longer reports "Invalid attack".

**Attacks wake sleepers.** `Damage` wakes every sleeper within `promiseHearingMetres` (30 m) of the victim, who records "Woken by a struggle". Gunshots already wake everyone within `noiseRange` (550 m).

**Keeping watch.** `PolarActionKind.Watch` is appended to the enum; others see it as "Keeping watch". A watch lasts 2, 4 or 8 hours near an accessible hut.
- **Ending:** it ends with "Watch interrupted" when the watcher swims or drops below 34 °C or satiety 8. It also ends when they leave the hut or are hit.
- **Waking others:** `WakeSleepers` works only while watching. It wakes everyone asleep within 30 m with the same "Warned by watch" record NPC watchers use.
- **Noticing danger:** `WatchDanger` reports someone newly in view, a visible raised rifle, or a gunshot since the last look.
- **Speed:** the controller runs a watch at `sleepMultiplier` while the watcher stands still with no panel open. Danger sets `WatchAlert`, which pauses the speed-up without ending the watch.
- **Keys:** V wakes sleepers, C continues, X stops. Raising, firing or reloading a weapon ends the watch first.

Harm during a promise is a breach: `Damage` first marks every active promise of that pair broken, then applies the harm, so the victim (and NPC retaliation) can respond normally. Both participants remember the breach. A physical sight witness must perceive both people; without knowledge of the private promise, that witness records a conflict rather than learning hidden terms. The breaker's promises with other people stand. `ProtectionWithdraw` needs the other person visible within `promiseHearingMetres=30`; it ends every active promise between the two after `protectionWarningHours=1`. Only actual awake listeners receive the announcement.

A completed search makes the searcher remember "Read notes"; the hut window lists notes after a search, newest first, as "You" or the writer's name with day and time.

**Clearing together.** Each clearer adds `factor(n)/n` of the solo rate, where n counts living actors currently clearing that site and the factor is 1, `jointClear2=1.7` or `jointClear3=2.2` (three or more). When the entrance opens, every clearer's action completes. The HUD shows the site's clearance rather than one person's elapsed time.

**Weather.** `PolarWeather.Next` draws each four-hour period from the seed. A blizzard starts with probability `blizzardStartChance=0.13` and lasts 2–4 periods (`blizzardPeriodsLeft`), followed by one forced calmer period (`blizzardCooldown`); otherwise snow 2/5, clear 3/5. Over 20,000 periods this gives about 28% blizzard, 29% snow and 43% clear, close to the earlier weather. Wind direction may still turn during a storm.

**UI.** The HUD drops the water figure and shows "Thirsty — reach a lit fire" below 25 and "Dehydrated — rest at a lit fire" at 0. The backpack shows load in kilograms, fuel and food in days, and "Eat one" for frozen rations. The hut window shows the stove as Broken, Usable (from outside), Lit or Out; fuel in days; "Pour … days of fuel into flask", "Add … days of fuel to stove" and "Put out stove"/"Light stove"; and the notes column with a 200-character field. The NPC panel shows "Promise with … until day D, HH:MM", "Promise withdrawn by … — ends …", "Promise broken by …" or "No promise", with Propose/Accept, Withdraw and Decline. `PolarMvpController.Update` ignores H, I, M and Tab while a text field in the NPC or hut panel has keyboard focus (`PolarMvpUi.IsTyping`); focus resets when the panel changes.
