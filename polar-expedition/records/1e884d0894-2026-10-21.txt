## Distant cliff band normals
<!-- project-update-log:2026-10-21:cliff-band-normals -->

- Reason：The user asked to confirm the faint stripes inside distant cliff rock bands and to fix them unless they had improved; they were unchanged from er-01.
- Implementation：
  - Distant meshes difference their normals a whole vertex spacing either side on each axis (PolarWorldStreamer.MeshNormal, PolarWorldController.Normal with per-axis offsets; the morph's coarse grid and fans too); near chunks keep 0.15. Same four height samples.
  - Distant blocks trade columns for rows until cells are about square at the same vertex count (Grid); resolution levels, refinement and the cliff band's blocks are unchanged, and the band measures each axis with its own spacing.
- Validation：
  - Three assemblies compile. Six genuine pairs (four relief views from the vantage, the camp's near and middle views) with the five-areas adapter.
  - Package 0.6.31-test sq-01 against er-01, alternating on the same 120 Hz display, two runs each: new game playable 62.8 and 54.6 s (55.7, 56.5), refined 249.1 and 223.5 s (295.5, 266.0); Continue playable 21.7 and 19.9 s (21.1, 20.6), refined 209.4 and 195.0 s (214.6, 242.4); P95 while refining 8.34 ms in all four (8.34-12.49), worst frame 333-567 ms (404-650). The 62.8 s run lost its time in world generation, which this change does not touch.
- Decisions and scope：Diagnosed with temporary shader views: not the rock texture, shadow maps, screen-space occlusion, illumination or reflections, smoothness from coverage, or the terrain itself (true heights are smooth apart from one 4-5 m step); the cause was distant-mesh vertex normals differenced 0.15 lattice units either side, which alias a face narrower than the mesh spacing.
- Remaining work：Fainter ribs remain on the upper part of the relief streak; removing them needs a smoother normal kernel than a box difference, cheapest from the mesh's own grid heights.

## Thick snow at every distance: snow edges, Cliffs_01 rock, rock caps, micro and contact shadows
<!-- project-update-log:2026-10-21:thick-snow-s1 -->

- Reason：The user: the ground snow reads thin, like a paper on the ground, and slope rock shows an unnatural repeating frieze; they supplied PBR Rocks - Nature Pack 3.0. All snow was pixel shading, so rock and bare patches were colour changes at the same height, and the slope-rock map's large rectangular strata repeated every 7 m.
- Implementation：
  - Slope rock and every kind-7 rock use owned 2K copies of Cliffs_01 (height in the albedo's alpha) instead of T_beach_cliff_01; rock luma .248 to .186.
  - Rock/snow and bare/snow edges follow the rock's and gravel's height; snow fills cracks on faces that look up; rock and bare ground darken in a band under the snow edge, which sits at mask level .3.
  - Slightly rougher snow, steeper sastrugi lee slopes and deeper hollows; rock caps swell up to 4 cm with a darker band under them; micro shadows and sun contact shadows.
- Validation：
  - Three assemblies and the Polar, habitat and particle shaders compile without errors; 19 genuine pairs from PolarFiveAreasCapture (camp, relief) and PolarArtCapture (b3-ground, b5-land), all captures PASS.
  - Package 0.6.32-test sn-01: gameplay 339, physical 831, performance 59, weather 80, weather-resume 10 and ui 382 pass; peak 6.14 GB; performance-mode P95 16.68-16.70 ms as fv-01; uncapped Editor P95 mean 9.86 to 10.13 ms over 18 rows.
- Decisions and scope：
  - Stage 1 of four works in shading and the rock map, which shows at every distance; a raised layer would be below a pixel in these frames. Cap coverage stays as it was (the boulder was already mostly white).
  - Two snow-only views (blizzard, looking down) stay below the comparison threshold and are kept as stability evidence.
- Remaining work：A fine diagonal hatch on steep faces 150-250 m away; frozen pond edges stay flat; open snow needs the raised layer, relief and footprints of Stages 2-3.

## Distant normals from the grid's own heights
<!-- project-update-log:2026-10-21:cliff-sobel-normals -->

- Reason：0.6.31-test left faint ribs on distant sheer cliff faces (the relief streak 300-590 m from the vantage): distant normals were a box difference of one vertex spacing, which lit a face narrower than the spacing steep or flat by where it fell between vertices.
- Implementation：
  - Distant grid normals come from the block's own positions and two rings around it (PolarWorldStreamer.Surround, GridNormal): central differences of the grid smoothed 1-2-1 both ways, about one height sample per vertex instead of five.
  - Stitches, fan centres and gap-outline points interpolate the grid's normals over its triangles (MeshNormal, OffGrid); the morph rebuilds the coarse mesh's normals the same way; vertices on the near window's edge take the near chunks' 0.15-unit normal. PolarWorldController.Normal keeps only its 0.15-unit form.
- Validation：
  - Three assemblies compile. 13 genuine pairs from PolarFiveAreasCapture (camp and relief views; five of them light the normals alone through a temporary shader switch, removed afterwards). In Play Mode the streamer's normals match an independent computation within 0.16 degrees over 1,652 vertices; blocks at the same spacing share identical edge normals, including across the angular seam.
  - Package 0.6.33-test cn-01 against sn-01, alternating, two runs each: refinement complete 140.0-143.2 s new game (225.1-245.7) and 109.4-109.7 s Continue (197.1-217.1); playable 51.6-53.0 s (53.9-57.5) and 16.9-17.7 s (20.0-20.8); P95 while refining 8.34-8.35 ms in all eight; worst frame 329-471 and 392-438 ms (575 plus one 17.6 s stall, and 333-367 ms).
- Decisions and scope：
  - A 3x3 Sobel kernel (one ring) was built and captured first; it left a fainter chain of beads along the face in a top-down shading of the streak's block, so the kernel spreads each difference over two spacings. A sheer face narrower than a vertex spacing now shades softly; the rock band's colour still marks it.
  - Player modes were not rerun: only distant meshes' normals change, and the acceptance's distant checks count resolution levels, which are unchanged.
- Remaining work：Blocks at different spacings (a 256 beside a 128) still differ slightly at their shared edge, as before. The sides of the cliff band 200-400 m away show feathered teeth in the normal view, from the band's vertex colour and the rock map.

## Thick snow Stage 2: a drawn snow layer 0.10 m above the ground
<!-- project-update-log:2026-10-21:thick-snow-s2 -->

- Reason：Stage 2 of the thick-snow plan (approved; the user asked to continue through every stage): the ground snow read as paint on the ground; Stage 2 gives it height near the player, so boots, tents, crates and rocks stand in it.
- Implementation：
  - A depth of world position only (PolarSnowDepth: the painted snow's cover, slope, rocky-tile, knoll-rock and river-ice terms with an exposure noise, up to 0.10 m; 0 round huts, in the hub sectors and on pond and gap outlines) is baked into terrain vertices' tangent w and lifted by PolarMeshModification.hlsl in every pass; near chunks everywhere, distant blocks on the window cut.
  - Pitched and pitching tents clear it through a global array (PolarSnowShelter); items put down lie on it (Seat with SnowAt, display only) and are seated again when tents change it; narrow cracks lie in it; drift runs 0.10 m higher; OnLocalCutBoundary also accepts the window column at x = Width.
- Validation：
  - Two adversarial review rounds (16 and 11 confirmed findings) fixed before the build; three assemblies and every Polar and habitat shader compile; 29 genuine pairs (five-areas and polar-art), all captures PASS, no seams in the 600-frame level-of-detail walk.
  - Package 0.6.34-test sd-01: gameplay 339, physical 831, performance 59 (P95 16.67-16.71 ms), weather 80, weather-resume 10 and ui 382 pass, peak 6.16 GB; alternating with cn-01, weather GPU and loading times are unchanged.
- Decisions and scope：0.10 m keeps within the earlier 0.15 m cap for walkable visual snow; the relay's 2 cm bound on visual-only snow no longer applies to the ground's own layer. Bodies, scatter, escarpment proxies and stuck harpoons are not lifted.
- Remaining work：Stages 3-4 (deep snow with relief and footprints near the player; pack rocks). The change is subtle from 30 m on; around the inner ring a near chunk released by the window drops its layer 20-55 m away.

## Distant cliff band edges
<!-- project-update-log:2026-10-21:cliff-band-edges -->

- Reason：After 0.6.33-test the sides of the distant cliff band 200-600 m away still showed feathered dark teeth: rows of horizontal spikes and two long fingers on the cliff by the camp, toothed sides on the relief streak.
- Implementation：
  - PolarWorldStreamer.CliffBand fades the band's strength linearly over its three vertex spacings, which the distant mesh's triangles interpolate exactly (a smoothstep fade drew one tooth per row).
  - SurfaceColor writes the strength as negative vertex R (0 to -1): as .86-1 the band also read as a rocky tile, whose noise patches drew the fingers. Knoll cores keep .86-.98.
  - PolarGroundSurface's band coverage rises over a quarter of the strength, centred at .35, with a smaller slow-noise shift; the rock edge rule is unchanged, so other rock looks as before. Package 0.6.35-test, build cb-01.
- Validation：
  - Three assemblies compile. 12 genuine pairs from PolarFiveAreasCapture (camp and relief views); before = the thick-snow session's captures of 2dc657b3, after = the same views with the fix.
  - cb-01 against sd-01, alternating (cb, sd, sd, cb), one run each twice: refinement complete 147.0-148.6 s new game (145.8-147.1 s) and 110.2-112.4 s Continue (111.3-113.5 s); playable 53.4-54.4 s (53.4-54.3 s) and 17.4-18.3 s (17.9-18.0 s); P95 while refining 8.34-8.35 ms in all eight. A first round in the opposite order ran while another session compiled and is not counted.
- Decisions and scope：
  - Diagnosed with a temporary shader switch (band value, slope coverage, combined coverage, rock with and without the map height, contour views): not the normals or the slope term.
  - One value per vertex serves weak and strong bands alike, so six coverage variants were captured on every camp and relief view: centred at .43 a steep rise broke the range view's weak ridge band into fragments; .35 keeps it continuous, with strong bands about 1.5 m wider each side at a 6 m spacing.
  - A global edge change (constant sharpness, half the map height beyond 40 m) was tried and dropped: it removed faint slope rock near the camp and made rock patches 2-6 km away blocky.
  - Player modes were not rerun: only distant meshes' colours and the ground shader's band line change, and the acceptance's distant checks count resolution levels, which are unchanged.
- Remaining work：A band still changes width at a border between a 256 and a 128 block, as before; the faintest cliff lines (sheerness under about .35) show no rock at a distance, where they showed scattered streaks.

## Thick snow Stage 3: deep snow, relief and footprints near the player
<!-- project-update-log:2026-10-21:thick-snow-s3 -->

- Reason：Stage 3 of the thick-snow plan (the user asked to complete every stage): Stage 2's 0.10 m layer was still subtle; the snow needed real depth, relief and tracks near the player.
- Implementation：
  - Open snow is drawn 0.15-0.45 m deep by exposure to the wind on every terrain vertex (PolarSnowDepth), thinning over 3 m to pond outlines; the distant morph carries the coarse depth.
  - A tessellated twin of the ground (PolarGround.shader, generated by PolarGroundTwinAuthor) draws sastrugi crests, grain, edge steps and exact tent pits within 18 m (12 m in a blizzard); its shadow pass is not tessellated. Gap cutting keeps whole any triangle a gap does not touch, and the relief stays flat at gap and pond edges, so T-junctions cannot open.
  - PolarSnowTracks: a toroidal footprint field round the camera stamped by boots within 18 m and refilled by falling snow, never saved; setting Footprints Off / Player / Everyone; the relief flattens round items, lying people and cracks; the dead and sleepers outdoors lie on the snow. GDD 3.4.2 and 6.7 amended.
- Validation：
  - Spec chosen from three designs by judges; two code reviews; offline fxc compile of every pass; 42 genuine pairs with a capture-only tracks group; 0 holes in both probes; settings check 72 pass.
  - Package 0.6.36-test: gameplay 339, performance 59, weather 80, weather-resume 10 and ui 382 pass on st-01, physical 831 on st-03 after fixing a window-edge sample; against cb-01, weather GPU +0.6 ms at most and loading within its gates.
- Decisions and scope：GPU: the spec's full reach, 12 px segments and tessellated shadows cost +1.5 to +3.8 ms; fallback F1 (18/12 m, 20 px) and a plain shadow pass bring the Editor A/B to +0.3 to +0.95 ms (the trail rows about 0.1 ms over the 0.8 ms budget).
- Remaining work：Stage 4 (pack rocks in the world). Crest shadows are short; the front-page run with the Footprints stepper check was not run (it needs the window in front).
