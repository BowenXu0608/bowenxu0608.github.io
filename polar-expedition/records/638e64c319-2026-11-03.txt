## Thin ice: pond cost and load soak
<!-- project-update-log:2026-11-03:pond-cost-soak -->

- Reason：M4 of the deep-water plan: gate the breakthrough's cost and show that ponds load and unload without growth.
- Implementation：The performance mode (PolarMvpAcceptance.PondSoak.cs) measures pond 33 intact from its bank, breaking through until 6 s after, and quiet 9 s after, uncapped, since 60 fps pacing makes FrameTimingManager count the GPU's idle gaps (+6.6 ms paced, about 0.2 ms uncapped); then it visits that pond and one 250 m away 30 times each. The soak judges private memory by the floor of the last five visits, since one sample read a passing peak as growth in dm-05.
- Validation：Gameplay 390 on dm-05 and performance 72 on dm-06 (Logs/PolarV7/0.6.102-test). Breaking: frame P95 11.46 ms, GPU P95 +0.25 ms and main thread +0.21 ms over the quiet pond; +40 MiB private and +28 MiB render textures. Soak: every count and native memory +0, private floor +4 and +21 MiB.
- Remaining work：No paired runs against 0.6.95-test, which cannot run the scene; the runner's 1600x900 window; the front page's save-handling mode not rerun (v20 fixture, rule unchanged).
- Visual evidence:Test code only: the performance mode gains the pond cost gate and a two-pond load soak; nothing the player sees changes, and dm-05 draws exactly what dm-03 draws.

## The tent from Polar Habitat v1.1 (habitat plan M3)

- Reason：M3 of the habitat plan: the Polar Habitat v1.1 two-person tent replaces the code-built tent, with its flaps blending closed, a canvas settle sound, and M2's two heater faults fixed.
- Implementation：The pack tent (Resources/Polar/Camping/TwoPersonTent, PolarHabitatAuthor.BuildTent) turned half round, stood up from the pitch take's end, with M2's heater, the camp kettle, tin and sack and the pack's bags at the beds' places; front ridge guys stripped; canvas double-sided; the pack's flap meshes rewritten (hems, ties and eyelets rigid to the closed edge, open cloth under the gable, 3 cm out of the posts) and the front hems and groundsheet extended; flaps blend to Closed over 0.6 s with grips on the free edge; Tent/Settle as a flap seats; the open door's pane no longer glows and the burner mouth's seam line is gone; the woven canvas, weathering and STP bag removed.
- Validation：Shared rules 2748; gameplay 390, weather 80 and ui 463 on hm3-01; ui 463 on hm3-02 and on hm3-03 (final), which differ only in the tent's flap, wing and groundsheet meshes (Logs/PolarV7/0.6.103-test). 43 five-areas cases before and after; a 32-agent review with a second reader per fault and a 14-agent recheck of the final set.
- Remaining work：The rope ties dip about 1 cm behind the closed canvas at their bend; the world group's tent cases are staged with the talk camera on Adler (before and after alike); M4 huts and Pole Station, M5 release.

## Intact pond ice: clear, cracked, floating (thin-ice plan M3)
<!-- project-update-log:2026-11-03:intact-ice-look -->

- Reason：M3 of the thin-ice plan: intact pond ice read as a painted floor with Voronoi cell lines and plate seams, not as clear, cracked ice over deep water (concepts 03, 09, 10).
- Implementation：Each pond gets its own refrozen crack network (PolarIceMap, worker thread, deterministic), drawn as sheets through the ice's 6 cm along the refracted view; white ice along the bank, clear ice darkening to near black 6 m out, bubbles in four layers in patches, clear ice .95 smooth in screen-space reflections; cell lines, plate seams and _PolarDamage removed. Package 0.6.104-test, build ii-02 (ii-01 plus a cheaper crack loop). Render only.
- Validation：Seed 777 passes 1104 world checks, one new for the maps; gameplay 390 and performance 72 on ii-02, continue 36 and weather 80 on ii-01 (Logs/PolarV7/0.6.104-test). ii-01 failed the pond gate (+2.54 ms GPU P95 breaking through over quiet, the crack loop on the undersides); ii-02 passes at +0.32 ms. Eleven before/after pairs of b4-intact-ice (pond 33, seed 777) inspected.
- Decisions and scope：Exact crack segments per 1 m cell instead of a distance field (hairlines stay sharp); no pond floor (20 m water since v23), a shore-distance texture instead; no clear coat (the hand-ported Lit has it only in ray tracing); value noise on two grids 37 degrees apart (one grid showed square cells).
- Remaining work：Against 0.6.102-test's run, not paired, pond 33 is 1.3 ms (bank) to 2 ms (in the pond) slower on the GPU; a paired run has not been made. Next: the plan's M4 (bending and flooding), M5 (the shore), then the pond hole rims.

## The island's coast and the sea
<!-- project-update-log:2026-11-03:coast-and-sea -->

- Reason：The user asked for the island's edges, the ice holes and the sea to look like Codex's ten ice-edge concepts; M1 of the adjusted plan covers the coast and the sea (E10).
- Implementation：
  - At the sea the ground draws dark wet rock and stones under the water, a wet band that surges with the weather and an icy glaze above it, and the drawn snow (and SnowAt) thins to the waterline (PolarIceEdge.hlsl, PolarCoast.Snow).
  - Broken shore ice crowds the waterline: slanted plates 15-40 cm thick of many widths with open water between, many drifted and turned, each with a bent middle row, and young pancake plates loose 1-25 m out, built on a worker within 700 m (PolarCoast, PolarIceEdge.Coast and Bay, kind 9 IceEdge); coastal bays get the same along their walls.
  - The nearest 512 loose plates ride the HDRP ocean's swell (WaterSimulationSearchJob) and eight shore-wave foam decals break along the nearest waterline (SeaShore.mat). Render only; colliders, rules, saves and world hashes unchanged.

## Thin ice bends and floods under load
<!-- project-update-log:2026-11-03:ice-bending-flood -->

- Reason：M4 of the thin-ice plan: a loaded plate should bend as one sheet and flood before it breaks; the drawn cap was a rigid dish and water only seeped as a painted stain.
- Implementation：Loaded weak plates are drawn bent by the vertex stage (D e^-r (cos r + sin r), four bowls from PolarThinIceRuntime.LateUpdate, 1.5x the rules' sag), drawn without their caps' rigid sag so seams meet; plate tops in 0.3 m rows; a dark mirror film draws the flood with the sheet held 3 mm over the water; caps draw camera motion vectors; the CPU dish and the seep are gone. Render only; package 0.6.106-test, build ib-01.
- Validation：Capture b4-intact-ice PASS 21 before (ib-before) and after (ib-after2); gameplay 390, continue 36 and performance 72 pass on ib-01 (Logs/PolarV7/0.6.106-test/ib-01); pond 33 GPU P95 8.91 ms intact, 11.38 breaking, 11.00 quiet against ii-02's 9.23, 13.17, 12.86 (not paired); the 30-visit soak holds at +0.
- Decisions and scope：HDRP's pond water over the bent sheet showed the rows as a pale faceted polygon from a low eye (ib-after), so the sheet stops 3 mm over the water and the film is the flood (ib-after2).
- Remaining work：No motion vectors for the bend itself; the boot is not planted in the bowl. M5 (the shore) is next.
