# 2026-10-30 Engineering log

## Stove light from its fire window
<!-- project-update-log:2026-10-30:stove-window-light -->

- Reason：The user's 0.6.84-test playtest: the tent stove's light glowed above the stove's corner, not from its fire, and a closed stove can light a room only through its window.
- Implementation：The tent stove lights the tent from its window: a wide soft-shadowed spot 5 cm out from the mica pane and a dim shadowless fill mid-tent, flickering with a now animated glass; a hinged door swings open on the light and refuel gestures and the fire flares while it is open (PolarTentView, PolarTentParts, PolarPhysicalGesture). The hut stove's lamp already stood at its glass and stays; it and the room fill follow the glass's flicker and flare when lit or fed (PolarArtHutDressing.FireWave, Flare; PolarHdrpPresentation, PolarSiteView).
- Validation：Gameplay 382 and weather 80 pass on sl-01; ui 448 passes on sl-05 (Logs/PolarV7/0.6.85-test).
- Decisions and scope：The hut lamp was not moved: it already stood in front of the glass.

## Sleep closes the eyes instead of a page
<!-- project-update-log:2026-10-30:sleep-eyes-close -->

- Reason：The user's 0.6.84-test playtest: the full SLEEPING page over a close-up of the bag looked odd; the user asked for sleep as in Minecraft.
- Implementation：Sleep has no page: the view turns up toward the roof over 1 s, two soft lids close over 1.6 s, the screen goes black with the day, the clock and X  Wake up faint at the bottom, and ambience falls by half. The lids open over .8 s at 06:00 or on X, or in .25 s with a start when harm, a shot or a struggle wakes the player (PolarBodyFeedback.Lids, PolarMvpUi.BuildSleep, PolarAudio.Dim). Sleep still runs the world at 10x.
- Validation：Gameplay 382 and weather 80 pass on sl-01; ui 448 passes on sl-05, whose talk scene now waits for the player to stand up from the cot (Logs/PolarV7/0.6.85-test).
- Decisions and scope：The user kept sleep at 10x; presentation only.

## Louder steps and breath, wind ducking
<!-- project-update-log:2026-10-30:louder-steps-breath -->

- Reason：The user's 0.6.84-test playtest: breathing and footsteps were too quiet to hear.
- Implementation：Steps are about 8 dB louder (walk .03 to .08, run .06 to .15, gear .035 to .06, explorers' steps in proportion) and breathing 7-8 dB louder (panting, cold breath, sleeping breath, which now alternates both takes); the wind beds dip 4 dB for .35 s while the player's own steps or panting play (PolarFootsteps, PolarActionSounds, PolarAudio.Own, PolarAtmosphere).
- Validation：Gameplay 382 and weather 80 pass on sl-01; ui 448 passes on sl-05 (Logs/PolarV7/0.6.85-test). Loudest-100 ms levels are recorded in polar-audio.md.
- Remaining work：A listening reel in medium snow and a blizzard for the user's verdict.
- Visual evidence:Sound levels only: no pixel changes; levels are computed from the shipped clips in polar-audio.md.

## World v20: half the area, 7 huts a district, relief around a middle base
<!-- project-update-log:2026-10-30:world-v20 -->

- Reason：The user's 0.6.84-test playtest: halve the area so people meet more often, keeping 7 supply points a district with the same resources; too much flat ground, so the plains should lie between lower and higher land; bare tundra on flat ground in a blizzard; straight snow and cliff lines. Earlier records are deleted, not carried over.
- Implementation：World v20 (recipe 9): v19's huts at 0.707 of its scale less 3 a district, 156 sites with unchanged totals; rolling plains, basins, massifs 1.9 times higher, hummocks, and the 500 and 250 m octaves 3.5 times as high (2.25 times along the walks; PolarLandforms FineGain20, FineWalk20; PolarHills fine); crest-only bare ground; curving clearance edges and wandering rivers; earlier saves and world caches deleted by a built game's front page (PolarSaveService.DeleteEarlier); v20 checks for ponds (11), lees (10 steps) and cracks (5.5 m).
- Validation：Twenty seeds pass (Logs/PolarCompactWorld/v20-twenty-fine3): flat ground 6.3% (v19 7.6%), a walk's climb 29 m (27), local relief 39 m (39), highest point 368 m (309). Gameplay 382, physical 3693, continue 34, performance 59, weather 80 and ui 448 pass on wv-07; the front page passes 88 on wv-09 until exclusive full screen, which needs the window in front; a new expedition is ready in 48.7 s (0.6.85-test 47.2 s).
- Decisions and scope：Local relief stays about v19's: at half the area the same climbs are steeper, and every stronger variant cut off a pass, a door, a district's lees or ponds, or failed a route capsule under the explorers' 0.7 limit.
- Remaining work：The NPC three-day diagnostic needs ANTHROPIC_API_KEY; the front page's display checks need the window in front; more relief needs a steeper walking limit or finer cells along the walks.

## Hard walking: slopes, wind, drifts, gusts and falls
<!-- project-update-log:2026-10-30:hard-walking -->

- Reason：The user's playtest of 0.6.84-test asked to show how the environment makes walking hard (falls with a hurt sound, lying for seconds and getting up; the same in blizzards) and agreed to fall damage.
- Implementation：
  - Climbing, a wind ahead and drifts deeper than 0.2 m slow walking for the player and every explorer, never below .3 of the speed (PolarWalkEffort).
  - Gusts shove the player downwind and lean the view; a blizzard's strongest gusts knock walkers down at most once a minute; ice slips runners and people going down it.
  - Drops over 3.5 m lay the body down and cost 7 health a metre past 3, scaled by the ground; the fallen lie 1.5-4 s and get up, heard on the ground, the body and pain; a fall is not an attack and a fatal one is a Fall death.
- Validation：
  - Shared rules 2726 pass (20 new); physical 3693 and ui 448 on hw-01; gameplay 382, continue 34, weather 80 and performance 59 on hw-02, which changes only two test checks that assumed the old speed (Logs/PolarV7/0.6.87-test).
  - A 7 m drop onto snow costs 17.2 health; walking across a blizzard's wind as a gust peaks covers 10.3 m in 4 s (19.6 m before); the performance walk into a snowstorm's wind takes 2166 frames (1448).
- Decisions and scope：Replaces 'steep ground does not slow them' (October 17) and GDD 3.4.1's exclusion of fall damage (docs/design-decisions.md, top section).
- Remaining work：Explorers' walk playback does not slow with effort, so their feet may slide into a blizzard; the three-day NPC diagnostic needs ANTHROPIC_API_KEY.
