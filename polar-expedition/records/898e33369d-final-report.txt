# Frame budget (16.67 ms): final report (November 14, 2026)

The Windows standalone game against a 16.67 ms frame at 2560 by 1440, High. Baseline 0.6.154-test (`to-01`); final candidate
0.6.155-test (`fb-02`). Every experiment, kept or not: [loops.md](loops.md).

## Result

- **Benchmark.** Every segment's P95 is at or under 16.67 ms in every run of the candidate (on `to-01` the pond reached
  16.67 and the hut 16.44, with no margin).
- **Actual play.** Three of the four walked routes pass with margin. The hut at night passes as a whole (16.61 ms) and has
  two steps over: look round 16.92 ms and go out 16.72 ms. The measured fix lowers distant-terrain detail; the user decided to
  leave it as it is.
- **Hitches.** In 88,089 route frames, 3 of 25 ms or more, none recurring at a step. In a live game day with explorers on
  the model, 1 game-owned frame over 25 ms in play (code compiled on first use) and 4 as the expedition appears: loading's
  last frame behind the loading page, then the play loop's first frames compiling (profiled in loop 12; not changed).
- **Picture, rules, saves.** Unchanged; the full acceptance suite passes on `fb-02`.

## Still over budget

| Step | P95 | Over by | Cause |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Hut at night, look round | 16.92 ms | 0.25 ms | Facing west (yaw 240 to 300 degrees) the whole island's distant terrain is submitted behind the hut's wall: 12.9 million triangles, about 2.4 ms of GBuffer for ground no pixel shows. Other headings run at 14 to 15 ms |
| Hut at night, go out | 16.72 ms | 0.05 ms | The same terrain in view through the door and on leaving |

Options measured (loop 10) and the decision:

| Option | Effect | Picture | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Halve the distance at which distant terrain drops detail (6, 16 and 28 km to 3, 8 and 14) | Inside the hut facing west 16.60 to 15.92 ms of GPU; pond bank 15.18 to 14.61; look round to about 16.2 ms | Lowers far-ground detail; crops read the same | **Left as it is (the user, November 14)** |
| Quarter it | 16.60 to 15.66; pond bank 14.29 | Lowers it further | Not taken |
| Cull terrain hidden behind nearer ground | Not measured | Unchanged | Not started: a new subsystem, a risk of far ground appearing late, and no help inside a hut |

## After this report: 0.6.156-test (`mr-04`)

The package now in `PolarPackage.cs` is `fb-02` plus loop 14's change to the model client, and the suite's live mode brought

## After this report: 0.6.157-test (`dr-01`)

The user then chose to fix both items this report left: the distant detail rings are halved (3, 8 and 14 km), after 27
same-session comparison pictures showed at most 0.31% of pixels differing, and the loading page stays up over the first
frames of play. The hut route's go out is under the budget (15.64 and 16.49 ms) and its look round is at the edge (16.55 and
16.73 ms, from 16.92); the pond's Benchmark P95 is 15.10 to 15.80 ms. Results and every picture:
[distant-detail-entry/results.md](../distant-detail-entry/results.md).

## Limits of these numbers

- The window must be in front: behind another window every segment reads about 1 ms slower (two Benchmark repeats that ran
  behind a Windows Security prompt are excluded and were repeated).
- The pond crossing was walked without the player's weight on the ice: every line through the middle of that pond touches a
  weak plate.
- The streaming route's walk back stops by time (200 s, 490 m): gusts and slopes slow and turn the walker.
- One machine, one seed (777), one resolution and preset.
