## Play at 240 Hz checked: the game clock keeps real time in fullscreen
<!-- project-update-log:2026-10-23:clock-240hz-0641 -->

- Reason：The user asked whether the game clock lags real time or stalls during normal play at 240 Hz (the weather test had seen 0.325 s reported in 0.5 s and 0.6-5 s stalls), and to fix it if it does.
- Implementation：The loading profile gains a clock probe (-polarClockProbe <seconds>, PolarLoadingProbe): after entering a new expedition it plays unpaused and writes real time, Unity's unscaled and scaled time, the world clock and each frame's real length to <profile>-clock.json. Package build wt-04 (wt-03 plus the probe, which runs only when asked for).
- Validation：AC power, 240 Hz, new seed-777 expedition, 90 s from 5 s after entry (Logs/PolarClock/2026-10-23): borderless fullscreen 2560x1440 with vsync, the user's own settings, keeps game and world clock on real time (90.003 s in 90.005 s) at 67 fps on average, p95 22.5 ms, longest frame 103 ms, 48 frames over 50 ms during distant refinement; a 1600x900 window keeps unscaled time right (89.76 s) but deltaTime and the world clock sum to 82.9 s (8% slow).
- Decisions and scope：Nothing was changed in play: the user plays borderless fullscreen, where the clock keeps real time and no frame stalled beyond 103 ms.
- Remaining work：Windowed play at 240 Hz runs the game clock 8% slow (Unity rounds deltaTime down to whole refreshes for composed windows); the Player modes' background windows under-report more and stall 0.6-5 s.
- Visual evidence:A diagnostic probe and documents; nothing the player sees or plays changed.

## The game clock keeps real time in a window at high refresh rates
<!-- project-update-log:2026-10-23:windowed-clock-0642 -->

- Reason：The user asked to fix the slow game clock in windowed play at high refresh rates (at 240 Hz a 1600x900 window ran game time 8% slow).
- Implementation：Unity 6000.2 locks a composed window's Time.deltaTime to the display's refresh under vsync (a Unity bug, fixed in 6000.5). In a built Player in a window with vsync, PolarSettingsHost now sets Time.captureDeltaTime each frame to the real time the last frame took (at most Time.maximumDeltaTime), and leaves it off otherwise, so game time, physics and animation follow real time while vsync still paces the frames; settings are untouched. Package 0.6.42-test, build ck-01.
- Validation：Clock probe, AC power, 240 Hz, new seed-777 expedition, 90 s (Logs/PolarClock/2026-10-23): windowed 1600x900 before (wt-04) 82.9 s of game time in 90.0 s, after (ck-01) 90.004 s in 90.000 s at 120 fps on average, longest frame 50 ms; borderless fullscreen 90.003 s in 90.004 s before and after. Player modes on ck-01 at 240 Hz: weather 80 and ui 382 pass; the weather mode's door wait now reports 0.508 s in 62 frames.
- Decisions and scope：A Player-only clock correction for windows with vsync, to be removed when the project moves to Unity 6000.5.
- Visual evidence:Game speed, not appearance: the same clock probe measured before (wt-04) and after (ck-01); no frame looks different.

## Snow edges at bare crests taper instead of stepping along the terrain triangles
<!-- project-update-log:2026-10-23:snow-crest-edge-0643 -->

- Reason：The user asked to work on the leftovers, starting with the snow edge at sharp slope changes: at wind-scoured crests the drawn snow still ended in a toothed shelf along the terrain triangles.
- Implementation：PolarHills.Height takes an optional crease width; within that many metres of a ridge crease the ridge's slope term fades through zero instead of flipping sign. Only PolarLandscape.GroundCover passes one (CreaseMetres 3), so bare crest cover now fades over about 3.5 m and the drawn snow depth over about 1.2 m; heights, normals, navigation and world hashes are unchanged. A pinned snowedge capture group and a --diagnose-cover checker option. Package 0.6.43-test, build se-01.
- Validation：Seed 777 (--diagnose-cover): across a crest the slope fell from 0.317 to 0.056 and cover from 1.000 to 0.008 within 10 cm; now cover falls from 1 to 0 over about 3.5 m. Twenty v18 seeds pass with bare land 3.8-6.5% (was 3.9-6.5%, limit 7.5%). Three genuine pairs: the shelf with teeth becomes a soft, irregular edge. se-01: gameplay 339 and weather 80 pass.
- Decisions and scope：Wind-scoured crests keep their bare ground; only the crest edge softens (docs/design-decisions.md).

## The sea is drawn only beyond the coast and in coastal bays; inland hollows below sea level show as dry ground
<!-- project-update-log:2026-10-23:dry-hollows-0646 -->

- Reason：The user chose to draw the sea only beyond the coast and in the coastal bays: the infinite HDRP ocean (and the opaque sea plane before it) drew every inland hollow below sea level as open sea, swell and all, although it is dry land people walk on.
- Implementation：HDRP water exclusion (supportWaterExclusion on): PolarArtWater.Dry builds one mesh at load over the land inland of the coast's shelf (lattice rows below Height-70), 0.5 m above the sea and under every river and lake bed, with holes over coastal bays and leads, drawn with HDRP's own Hidden/HDRP/WaterExclusion shader on both faces, so no water surface is drawn over inland ground below sea level. Render only, every world version. A new hollow capture group. Package 0.6.46-test, build dl-01.
- Validation：Five genuine pairs on a new seed-777 expedition: the hollow 17 m below the sea's level, from 60 m above its rim and from 2 m above the sea's level, now shows its snow; the nearest lead, coastal bay and the sea off the nearest coast keep their water (their differences are wave motion). The exclusion pass takes about 0.006 ms of GPU time. dl-01: gameplay 339 and weather 80 pass (Logs/PolarV7/0.6.46-test/dl-01).
- Decisions and scope：Only drawing changes: water rules are as before (inland ground below sea level was already dry for walking and swimming).

- Reason：The user found the route flags too small and too solid, and asked for flags that wave in the wind.
- Implementation：Each route flag is a 3.2 m pole with a 1.1 by 0.7 m red cloth (was a 2.3 m pole with a rigid 0.42 by 0.26 m plate). PolarRouteFlag reshapes the cloth each frame while it is in view within 250 m: it streams downwind with the weather's wind heading, stands out further and flaps faster with stronger wind and gusts (45 degrees below the horizontal in a calm, near flat in a blizzard), and carries folds that run to its free end. Render only; the 600 m spacing of 0.6.47-test is unchanged. A flags capture group. Package 0.6.48-test, build rf-01.
- Validation：Four genuine pairs (the flag nearest a new seed-777 camp from 7 m and 40 m, clear weather and snowstorm) and a 60-frame clip from upwind in the snowstorm, in which the cloth changes shape from frame to frame. rf-01: gameplay 339 and weather 80 pass (Logs/PolarV7/0.6.48-test/rf-01).
- Decisions and scope：The cloth runs on game time, so it stops while the game is paused; flags out of view or beyond 250 m keep their last shape.
