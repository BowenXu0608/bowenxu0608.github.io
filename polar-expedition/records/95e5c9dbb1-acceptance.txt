# Fractured terrain and directional shelter acceptance

Approved October 1, 2026; final validation completed October 2. Windows non-Development 0.4.0, v9 terrain and isolated v3 expeditions. Published formal entry: `Builds/Windows/SurvivalLotteryMVP/SurvivalLotteryMVP.exe`.

Production input fingerprint: `d05a34e13e2f9d7d0231f8c768ae7fa1f1812202978f9d75470bdc53f9ca964a`. Seed 777 library `DD290A9A800E6DBD`; Unity world `20B5F61112A350BA`. Fresh final Player generation reproduces the independently captured gallery identities.

## Structure and variation

[Interactive twenty-seed gallery](maps/index.html) · [Original PNG archive](maps/20-map-screenshots.zip) · [All measurements](maps/statistics.json)

All twenty preregistered original seeds finish without screening. Each has 21 personal territories, 15 ordinary huts per territory and 324 sites. Minimum ordinary supply separation is 900.014–900.141 m. Graphs have 17–18 cycles and zero personal dead ends.

There are 2–4 jagged coastal cuts, one finite side branch per cut, and 3–8 interior holes. Cuts reach 35–70% of the personal radial thickness. Projected missing area is 8.43%–13.91%, including the intact central footprint in the denominator. Regional relief is 80.3–163.3 m; difficult interior coverage is 25.9%–28.4%.

Macro gaps are planned relative to regional dimensions; their contours, coverage and clearance are evaluated after projection. Narrow crests, saddles, slope checks and capsule clearance use physical metres. Difficult coverage includes mountain approaches with at least 15 m structure contribution and at least 0.45 additional travel cost, as well as blocked crests. It is not a claim that one quarter of the region is completely impassable.

All 420 regional audits and 6,500 key routes pass, covering 1,844,938 unique-per-world route edges with a four-metre capsule radius. This proves eight-metre terrain clearance; building doors retain their authored dimensions. All site entrances, starts, the station and rescue shore have stable routes that avoid thin ice. Mean start-to-station physical route length is 1.38–1.85 times straight distance.

All 190 pairs differ after rotation compensation: macro tile differences 77.6%–85.7%; sampled surface coverage differences remain nonzero after all 336 angular shifts. The sample is not a proof of uniform randomness or game balance. The fixed shared centre and 21 territories are intentional.

## Geometry and actual Player shelter

The route checker reads each captured production library; its .NET world digest differs from Unity because bitwise identity is a same-runtime contract. Reports are bound to the actual Unity library hashes, not claimed to have cross-runtime world identity. Exact movement-bank intersections also reject a five-centimetre slit between slope samples and a circular capsule grazing a tip.

The twenty data geometry reports cover the angular seam, the complete central surface, all internal holes, complete eight-metre saddles and three distinct wind responses at reachable positions. Actual final Player checks additionally inspect loaded terrain tops, raycast through an internal hole, reject missing ground and a cross-gap movement segment, and walk the real capsule through a saddle. Tops, explicit walls and bottoms are classified separately.

A real hole-bank check found nine float-precision edge slivers. The worker removes numerical faces below 0.005 square metres; the reproduced check retains 362,415 upward top faces with zero invalid centroids. Gap outlines, height recipes, ridges and navigation remain intact. This is numerical mesh cleanup, not terrain repair to satisfy connectivity.

Natural shelter is based on world heights and actual upwind distances. The final Player, unloaded NPC and loaded NPC give the same coefficient. The exact-position cache refreshes even for sub-metre movement; a previous two-metre history-dependent reuse was caught by the real Player test and removed. NPC decisions record their own observed lee and ignore distant memory; they do not receive the global audit catalogue. Shared rules pass 324 checks: at most 75% of weather-added cold is removed, with base cold, wet clothing, immersion and legacy v2 exposure retained. Natural lee supplies no heat, indoor drying or free recovery.

The copied validation save is aligned to the seeded daylight period before loading; [clock fixture receipt](results/wind-clock-fixture.json) preserves its original checksum and scope. This prevents a normal weather tick from replacing the selected test heading; assertions are unchanged. The final wind-direction fixture is preserved in [measured directions](results/wind-directions.txt). Wind headings follow seeded four-game-hour periods and survive saving. The HUD prioritizes building shelter indoors, then natural wind shelter outdoors; its [genuine comparison](../../2026-10-02/polar-wind-feedback/indoor-comparison.png) was inspected.

## Genuine visual comparisons and provenance

Five pre-change cases were captured before production edits: the full map, three terrain headings and blizzard. Final captures use the same seed, scene-camera transform relative to the generated spawn, headings, hour 6, FOV 60, maximum quality and 1600×900. Their global physical anchor changes with the generated layout; identical absolute world coordinates are not claimed.
