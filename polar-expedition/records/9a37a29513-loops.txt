# Frame budget (16.67 ms): experiment log

One entry per experiment, kept or not. Baseline: 0.6.154-test, `Builds/HdrpMilestones/to-01`, source `d71fc2e6e`.
Plan: the Benchmark's segments first (stage 1), then walked routes of actual play (stage 2).

## Conditions for every number here

- **Machine.** Intel i7-13700H (14 cores, 20 threads), NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4070 Laptop GPU (driver 32.0.16.1074), 15.8 GB of memory,
  panel 2560 by 1440 at 240 Hz, on mains, Windows power plan "Balanced".
- **Settings.** High preset, TAA, 100% render scale, Direct3D 11; the game's own borderless window at 2560 by 1440, kept in
  front (`Tools/polar_window_front.ps1`); vertical sync off and no frame cap while a part is timed, as the Benchmark sets them.
- **Timing.** Acceptance: the release Player's own whole-frame interval (`Time.unscaledDeltaTime`), per Benchmark segment.
  Diagnosis: a private copy of the Development Player with one-variable switches at fixed views, each timed A B B A A B B A in
  120-frame blocks after 40 unmeasured frames (`tools/PondLab.cs`), GPU time of each HDRP pass through `PolarPassProbe`.
  A lab number is the median of the four adjacent pair differences.
- **Host.** `Tools/polar_machine_monitor.py` beside every run. Other programs closed by the user. The desktop app that hosts
  this work starts a large `git diff` after some commands; a guard stops each as it appears (`tools/diffguard.py`).
- **Builds.** `to-01` (baseline, release). `fb-00` and `fb-00-dev`: the baseline's source plus the test options only (route
  recorder, pass probe); they draw as `to-01`. `fb-01` and `fb-01-dev`: the stage 1 candidate, 0.6.155-test.

round (16.92 ms P95) would come to about 16.2 or 16.0 ms.

Captures with the clock stopped (`data/rings-*-captures.jpg`: two of today's rings, then halved and quartered; crops of the far
ground at full size in `data/rings-west-crop.png` and `data/rings-pond-crop.png`). Pixels differing by more than 8 levels from

This lowers what is drawn, so it is the user's decision. The alternative that keeps the picture, culling terrain hidden behind
nearer ground, is a new subsystem with a risk of far ground appearing late, and does not help inside a hut, where the wall hides
the terrain.

**Decision (the user, November 14): "Leave it as is."** The rings stay at 6, 16 and 28 km and hidden terrain is not culled. The
hut route's look round and go out stay at their measured shortfall (16.92 and 16.72 ms P95, 0.25 and 0.05 ms over), and
0.6.155-test (`fb-02`) is the final candidate.
