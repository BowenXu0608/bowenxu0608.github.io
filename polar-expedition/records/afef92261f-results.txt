# A save's text: only the huts that changed are written again (November 11, 2026, test package 0.6.138-test)

The user's "Yes, do it here" after the live day on 0.6.137-test, where a save's payload text was the largest single cause of the slow frames left. Package `Builds/HdrpMilestones/pt-01`. The save file is byte for byte the same; no rule, save format or drawn pixel changes.

## What a save's text was made of

One live day's save (`Logs/SaveRate/fp-day-live/save/continue-v7.json`, 1,396,417 characters of payload):

| Part | Characters | Share |
|---|---|---|
| The 146 huts (`sites`) | 1,176,402 | 84.2% |
| of them, the 6,065 items in their 552 boxes | 967,558 | 69.3% |
| The 20 people (`actors`) | 205,111 | 14.7% |
| Things on the ground, rules, weather and the rest | 14,904 | 1.1% |

`JsonUtility.ToJson` wrote all of it on the main thread at every save: one string of 2.8 MB, about 11 ms. A hut's text changes only when someone opens a box, takes or puts something, uses its door or burns its stove.

## What changed

- **`PolarSaveService`** keeps each hut's text (`huts`) with a number mixed from every one of its saved fields (`Mark`: the hut's own fields, its boxes, their items and its notes, floats by their bits). At a save a hut's text is written again by `JsonUtility` only when its number has changed (`Kept`).
- **The rest of the state** is written by `JsonUtility` from a copy of the state's fields without its huts (`PolarGameplayState.WithoutSites`, so the state in play is never without them), and the huts' texts go in where `"sites":[]` stands (`Rest`, `cut`).
- **The worker** hashes and writes the pieces in order (`Hash` and `PolarSaveFile.WriteEnvelope` take the rest, the cut and the huts' texts), so the payload is never one string. Only the main thread writes the kept texts, after `Finish` has waited for the worker that reads them.
- **Two profiler markers**, `Polar.Save.Validate` and `Polar.Save.Text`, split the save's main-thread time.
- **The gameplay mode** changes every saved field of a hut, a box, an item and a note alone, found by reflection (52 changes), and requires the kept text to follow `JsonUtility`'s own each time, and the whole text put together from its pieces to be `JsonUtility`'s (`PolarMvpAcceptance.SaveTextFault`). A field added to one of the four classes and left out of `Mark` fails that check.

## The same bytes

`tests/PolarSaveTextProbe.cs`, run in the Editor without Play Mode over every kept save of the current rule version under `Logs` (1,535 files, 1.67 billion characters of payload; `data/probe-summary.txt`):

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| The whole text from its pieces equals `JsonUtility.ToJson(state)` | 1,535 of 1,535 |
| The kept hut text follows each of 52 single-field changes | 1,535 of 1,535 |
| `PolarSaveService.Write` gives the bytes of `JsonUtility`'s own envelope round `JsonUtility`'s own text with its SHA-256 (the states that pass today's validation), and `Read` takes the file | 627 of 627 |

- **Negative control** (`data/probe-control-summary.txt`): with `chamber` left out of `Mark`, the single-field check fails on `PolarItemInstance.chamber` in five of five files.
- **The control found a fault in the check itself.** Its first form passed with the field left out: each change's text had been written again because the change before it had moved the number. The check now keeps the hut's text as it stands before each change.

## Measured

A walking game day on Development builds (`Tools/polar_save_rate.py --walk --profile`: every explorer walks between its two nearest huts, no model, High at 1600x900, 179 saves in both runs). Before: `walk-after-2` on `fp-01-dev` (0.6.137-test without its last four small allocation fixes). After: `pt-walk` on `pt-01-dev`. Minutes 2 to 6, timed without call stacks, hold 48 saves.

| | 0.6.137-test | 0.6.138-test |
|---|---|---|
| A save on the main thread (`Polar.Autosave`), mean | 12.8 ms | 5.1 ms |
| of it, the text (`Polar.Save.Text`) | not split | 3.2 ms |
| of it, the validation (`Polar.Save.Validate`) | not split | 1.6 ms |
| The longest save frame's `Polar.Autosave` | 16.4 ms | 13.9 ms |
| The text's strings at one save | 2,994 KB | 578 KB, and up to 44 KB of huts written again |
| Managed allocation ("GC Allocated In Frame") | 18.0 KB a frame | 9.5 KB a frame |
| of it on the main thread | 15.6 KB | 7.5 KB |
| Collections on the main thread in the four minutes | 15 frames, 42 ms | 11 frames, 35 ms |
| Slow frames (over 25 ms) with a save as their largest part | 3 | 1 |
| Peak private memory | 4.98 GB | 5.02 GB |

- **In the Editor** (the probe, the largest kept state, 1,665,008 characters and 219 huts, mean of 30): the whole text 11.8 ms; the pieces with no hut changed 3.1 ms, with three huts changed 3.0 ms.
- **The walk's other slow frames are not saves.** The timed stretch has 73 frames over 25 ms where the earlier run had 44, and the whole day 236 where it had 231. The explorers' step is the largest part of 58 (41 before), a save of 1 (3), and 13 are the machine's (just-in-time compiling 6, frame pacing 4, the player loop 3; the user was working at the machine, and the window's focus changed during the run). The step's frames sit within a millisecond or two of 25 ms, so their count moves from run to run.
- **A live day** followed at the user's word ([results](../live-day-0138/results.md)): a save takes 4.2 ms of the main thread where it took 11.1 ms on 0.6.137-test, and none of the six frames over 25 ms in the timed four minutes is a save (two were).

## Verified

- **Offline.** The game's assembly compiles; shared rules 2750 (`Tools/PolarGameplayCheck`, with its envelope checks on the rewritten `PolarSaveFile`).
- **The full suite passes on `pt-01`,** 6,438 checks: gameplay 391 (the new check among them), physical 3959, physical-resume 18, continue 36, performance 79, buildings 395, fractured 31, handbook 353, weather 84, weather-resume 10, ui 463, handbook-revision 619 (`Logs/PolarV7/0.6.138-test/pt-01`). The continue, physical-resume and weather-resume modes read saves the new code wrote.
- **The front-page modes pass on `pt-01`:** front page 173, startup 4 and screen-args 3 (`Logs/PolarFront/0.6.138-test/pt-01`), the player's own saves and settings unchanged. They were run at the user's word once the suite was done; when it ended the user was working at the machine (another program's test running, 4.8 GB of memory available).

## Left

- A save still writes the text of everything but the unchanged huts (about 0.3 MB, mostly the people, whose records of places and people grow over the days) and validates the whole state: 5.1 ms of the main thread in the walking day, 4.2 ms in the live day.
- The explorers' step is the largest cause of slow frames in a walking day, as before.
