# 2026-11-04 Engineering log

## Snow on the pond ice and its shore
<!-- project-update-log:2026-11-04:pond-shore-snow -->

- Reason：M5 of the thin-ice plan: the 16-sided pond outline met the bank's snow in a hard line, and far ponds read as pale-blue discs.
- Implementation：A snow lip mesh in the ground's plain material carries the bank's snow 0.4-2 m onto loaded ponds' ice; the ice draws drift tongues and long streaks along the ground's wind, snow in about half the refrozen cracks, a mottled hoarfrost apron with glints and load cracks through the snow as wet grey lines; ponds past about 80 m are .62 smooth; distant ponds draw the same from uv0's shore distances with a painted lip. Render only (0.6.108-test, build ss-01).
- Validation：16 before/after pairs (seed 777, b4-intact-ice) inspected; gameplay 390, continue 36 and performance 79 pass on ss-01 (Logs/PolarV7/0.6.108-test/ss-01); pond 33 GPU P95 intact 8.14 ms, breaking 11.15 ms, quiet 10.97 ms; soak +0 meshes, materials, textures and objects.
- Decisions and scope：The lip holds the bank's snow depth 2.4 m up to the shore and falls as 1 - t^3, since the ground's drawn depth thins to nothing at the shore; the snow is procedural from shore distances, with no ice-map channel.
- Remaining work：Things put down within 2 m of the shore can sink into the lip; the lip hides a loaded plate's cracks under it. Next: the pond hole rims (E01, E02) when the user says.

## River, lake and lead shore ice: ice-edge plan M3
<!-- project-update-log:2026-11-04:shore-ice -->

- Reason：M3 of the ice-edge plan (concepts E03, E04): shore ice along rivers, lakes and leads was an even ribbon of 2 m quads with a gap after about every other one, with bare lake ends.
- Implementation：Shore ice is built by PolarIceEdge.Run (River and Lead profiles) as broken plates 6-16 cm thick with uneven widths, jagged water edges, cracks, rafted plates and snow by the bank thinning to clear ice; river plates sink to the water at the bank and open plates do not drift; ice runs round held lake ends; shadows within about 60 m; river pieces built on loading workers, a river each, while the leads are built. Render only (0.6.110-test, build ie-08).
- Validation：Gameplay 390 and weather 80 on ie-08 (Logs/PolarV7/0.6.110-test/ie-08b; a first ie-08 run stopped once when a hut door did not open within 5 s of real time, and passed on the rerun); performance 79 on ie-05, which differs only in the review's fixes: sea view median of 16 pairs +0.58 ms GPU P95, -0.26 ms main; new-game loading on ie-08 about 0.1 s of M3 on the main thread; seed 777 shore ice 324,529 vertices (1.29 times); 6 before/after pairs in b8-ice-edge.
- Decisions and scope：A review outside Unity found drifted and lifted plates showing their open sides and a load wait of 1.45 s, so open plates no longer drift, river plates' bank edges sink to the water, and each river goes to its own loading worker. River ends keep their 3 m taper: a level end row laid a sheet over lower land at one upstream end.
- Remaining work：M4 (gap walls: crevasses, bay ice fronts, lead rock), its own package.
