## Plan as approved

### Context

The user wants the fall through thin ice to look and feel real. Codex wrote the upgrade plan (`[user directory]\Downloads\PLAN (10).md`, 10 approved concepts C01-C10). In the plan's sequence, the ice cracks under load, its pieces fold into the water, the person goes briefly under among rising bubbles, and they surface and climb out onto ice that really holds them. The water is deep blue with no bottom.

What exists today:
- The peer session "Thin ice visual effect" is shipping the earlier plan's M1+M2 as `0.6.95-test` (build `ti-01`). Its Player suite is running now, and nothing is committed yet. It covers:
  - world v22, with water 3 cm under the ice;
  - a ragged rounded hole, with Voronoi pieces in their final pose;
  - one shared HDRP "Pond water" pool surface.
- The pool is 3 m deep in the rules. Under HDRP water it is *drawn* no lower than sea + 0.6 m, because the dry-land water exclusion (`PolarArtWater.Dry`, a stencil at -6.5 m) hides all water in front of a deeper floor.
- No surface renders underwater: `underWater=false` on all three surfaces, so water is invisible from below.
- No break event exists; consumers poll `Revision`/`swimming`, and the load point is not kept.
- The hands draw in a separate foreground pass that ignores world depth and water.

- The visible rim around a hole has no collider.

### Where this plan departs from Codex's text (and why)

1. **The sea inside a pond.** Codex asks for fragment clipping in "the ocean's owned water material". There is none: the sea uses HDRP's default water graph, and HDRP water graphs cannot discard fragments.
   - Instead, the exclusion is cut exactly to the footprint of each pond whose water lies above the sea plane (-7 m) plus a margin. There, the pond's own water surface is nearer than the sea and hides it.
   - While the eye is under water in a pond, the sea's surface is culled from the eye camera. HDRP skips a water surface whose GameObject layer is not in the camera's culling mask (`HDRenderPipeline.WaterSystem.cs:942-944`), so the sea moves to a layer of its own.
   - Ponds at or below the sea keep today's exclusion and owned water, with no underwater dip. M1 counts them on twenty seeds.
2. **One pond surface, re-anchored, not a second surface.** HDRP draws each custom-mesh renderer at its own transform, but it reads a pool's reference height (camera height and waterline), its `volumeBounds`, `underWater` and decal region from the surface itself. So the existing shared surface follows the *active pond*. Nothing is handed between surfaces and nothing is drawn twice.
   - The active pond is the one holding the player; otherwise the nearest loaded broken pond within 60 m, kept until 80 m.
   - If M1's two-pond capture shows a fault, fall back to Codex's dedicated surface.
3. **Light under the ice.** HDRP's underwater pass cannot read a per-pond mask. Instead:
   - shadowed volumetric light gives shafts through the opening (HDRP 17.2's voxelization already fogs underwater voxels, `VolumeVoxelization.compute:59-93`);
   - one scalar ambient dimmer (WaterRendering `ambientProbeDimmer`) follows how open the ice is around the eye.

   The world-aligned open-water mask is still built. It fades deformation under the ice, and the CPU water sample and the bubbles read it.
4. **Water deformation.** It uses HDRP's own "Sample Water Decal" graph in texture mode, fed by an owned 256² impulse texture. No new shader graph is needed. The CPU sample evaluates the same impulse function.
5. **The break point is saved.** It is one additive field per broken plate in `PolarIceSnapshot`, so a reloaded hole shows the same pieces the player saw. Older saves use the plate's hashed point.
6. **Paths and versions.** The repository is `D:\NewGame`, not `[user directory]/NewGame`. The depth change is world **v23**, since v22 ships in 0.6.95-test.

### M2 — Ice from every side, the underwater view, the dip and the hands → test package

**Support that matches the visible ice** (rules, render, climb)
- Move the hole contour into a pure `PolarThinIce.Hole(pond, plate)` in `PolarThinIceData.cs`. Today it is the rounded inset plus fBm in `PolarArtIcePondView.Rim`.
- Each rim strip belongs to the unbroken neighbour across its edge. Strips taper out within 0.6 m of an edge shared with another hole, so merged holes open as one.
- **Player:** each strip gets a `MeshCollider` with `PolarIceSupport{pond, neighbour}`, so standing on it loads the neighbour and `FindClimb` hits the visible edge.
- **Explorers:** a new `PolarThinIce.SupportAt(pond, x, z)` replaces the polygon lookup in `PolarMvpController.StepEnvironment`. `PolarThinIce.ClimbOut` targets the contour.
- Pieces stay non-supporting.
- Rebuild only the broken plate and its neighbours' strips when one breaks; today `Sync` rebuilds the whole pond.

**Ice geometry and materials** (`PolarArtIceCaps`, `PolarArtIcePondView`, `PolarData.hlsl`)
- Plates get an underside 6 cm down. Rim strips and pieces get top, bottom and fractured sides; cut faces reach the 6 cm thickness.
- **Wet band:** the top within about 12 cm of the water is darker and smoother. **Thin edges:** thickness transmission on the cut-face and piece materials only, with one owned diffusion profile. The main body stays opaque.
- **New mask data:** a dedicated uv channel; the r/g/b/a/uv0 meanings are kept.
- Regenerate the PolarGround twin if `PolarSurface.shader` changes.

**Underwater**
- `underWater` is on only while the eye is inside the active pond's true outline (`PolarThinIce.Contains`) and below its water.
- The sea's layer leaves the eye camera's mask at the same time.
- A WaterRendering override lowers `ambientProbeDimmer` by the open fraction within 6 m of the eye.
- Ice caps, strips and pieces cast sun shadows, so the volumetric shafts come through the opening only.
- The lens pass (`PolarLens.shader`, `PolarHdrpLensPass`) adds slight softness and distortion while submerged.
- There is no second blue fog.

**The dip** (`PolarBodyFeedback`)
- Trigger: the real pond entry in `PolarSurveyPlayer.Move` (the `Swimming` switch), which emits a `ThinIceVisualEvent`.
- Curve: an additive 1.2 s offset aimed at a height relative to the sampled surface — about −0.35 m held briefly, then back to the swim eye at +0.47 m. Mouse look and movement stay live.
- It is composed just before the feedback's final write, so the landing and limp terms cannot erase it.
- The rules' sight point (`PolarMvpController.ActorSightPoint(0)`) ignores the dip.
- Cleared on:
  - teleport (`PlaceAt`), restored swimming (`RestoreExposure`), quitting or a new world, and death;
  - an E climb: climbing is blocked until the dip has passed 0.8 s.
- It runs on game time, so pause freezes it.

**Hands** (`PolarWeaponView`, `PolarHdrpForegroundPass`)
- During entry, swimming and climbing, layer 10 joins the world camera's mask and the foreground pass skips the weapon view. The rig then draws once through world depth and water, with refraction, absorption and occlusion by the rim, at the world camera's field of view.
- Rig shadows are off in this mode. The switch happens while the film is strongest.
- Normal ownership returns on exit and in `OnDestroy`.

**Film:** a short water film on the lens, separate from gameplay wetness. It decays over 1.5 s, and sparse drops are gone by 4 s.

**Audio** (`PolarActionSounds`, `PolarBodyFeedback`, `PolarAudio`)
- An `AudioLowPassFilter` at the listener blends toward 800 Hz while the eye is submerged. UI sources set `bypassListenerEffects`.
- The underwater factor folds into the feedback's existing `PolarAudio.Dim` call.
- ColdBreath and Pant mute quickly while under.
- The player's gasp plays at real resurfacing, the `Resurface` event, instead of 0.5 s after entry; `Puff` breath comes with it. Breath is already silent with the mouth under water (`PolarBreath` L68).

### M3 — The continuous breakthrough → test package

**Events and samples**
- `ThinIceVisualEvent`: kind (LoadStart, Break, Entry, Resurface, FragmentImpact, Stroke), pond, plate, actor, sequence, presentation time, position, velocity and strength.
  - It is raised once from real transitions: `PolarThinIceRuntime` for load and break, `PolarSurveyPlayer` for the player's entry and resurfacing, `PolarNpcView` for explorers.
  - It is never saved and never changes survival.
- `WaterSurfaceSample PolarThinIceRuntime.Sample(pos)` returns the height, normal and velocity (level + impulses), whether the point is in a pond, and the ice cover from the mask.
- Per-pond render state (the existing `Loaded`) owns the mask, meshes, fragment state and water registration, and its `Dispose` releases them all.

**Fracture** (`PolarThinIceRuntime`, `PolarArtIcePondView.Fracture`)
- `SetLoad` takes the feet position, from both the player and explorers.
- When weight first comes onto a plate, its topology is computed from the seed, the plate and the quantised load point. That is the Voronoi network that exists today, seeded toward that point.
- Its edges become a 128² crack distance texture, built on a worker thread. The shader reveals it outward with stress, as straight cracks with kinks; this replaces `PolarStressFracture`'s curls and rings.
- At the existing break (thresholds unchanged), support goes at once. The already-cut pieces fold down and out over 0.2-0.5 s from the actual load point, then float.
- Saved and streamed-back holes are built settled, with no replay. The break point is saved.

**Floating**
- At most 24 large pieces near the event are simulated at 30 Hz with rendering interpolated:
  - four buoyancy samples on `Sample`, water drag and angular damping;
  - bounded impulses from bodies within reach;
  - a spring to their deterministic rest pose, so energy dies out and a reload matches.
- The other pieces keep today's sine `Animate`.

**Water interaction**
- Turn on `supportWaterDecals` in `PolarPipeline.asset` and `deformation` on the pond surface.
- One `WaterDecal` uses HDRP's sample decal material in texture mode, with a 16×16 m region anchored at the event or the player.
- An owned blit shader writes a 256² height texture: at most 16 damped radial impulses (entry 0.12 m; impacts; strokes), times the open mask, so nothing rises under intact ice or at the rim.
- The CPU `Sample` uses the same function and constants.
- The thresholded weather writes stay; nothing rebuilds a spectrum.

**Particles** (pooled, replacing the per-call `Splash` GameObject)
- Droplets: 48, ballistic, each living until it falls back to the water.
- Bubbles: 96, CPU-steered (`GetParticles`/`SetParticles`). They rise, stop under the ice's underside where the mask says ice, and spread sideways until open water. They draw before refraction, so the water attenuates them once.
- Sparse foam and slush around impacts.
- The nearby cap is 1,024; distant emitters lose budget first.

**Audio:** entry, stroke, piece impacts and resurfacing come from these events, using recorded library clips only; skip any sound with no fitting clip.

**Explorers:** their view's WaterFall entry raises the same events, with splash, bubbles, impulses and a gasp at their resurfacing.

### Risks to watch

- **Assumed water depth sorting.** The sea is assumed to sort behind a pond's water by depth. M1 checks this with a capture before the exclusion cut is trusted.
- **Lighting jump on the hands.** The hands change lighting when they move from the foreground pass to the world draw.
- **Near clip.** The near clip is 0.08 m in the world against 0.02 m in the foreground.
- **Build time.** Edits to `PolarData.hlsl` recompile every PolarSurface variant.
- **Peers.** Peers may hold Unity, so every Editor or Player run is announced in the notes.
