# 2026-11-06 Engineering log

## Hut timber from a CC0 plank set
<!-- project-update-log:2026-11-06:hut-timber -->

- Reason：Graphics core M6's timber step (G04.01): the pack's three timber sets were procedural, nearly flat grain, so hut walls, roof, floor and ceiling read as smeared khaki.
- Implementation：Seam-free weathered grain cut from Poly Haven's CC0 plank_flooring_03 (Tools/polar_hut_timber.py: gap-free plank middles laid side by side, strips at even brightness, the old sets' mean colour and smoothness) replaces TimberGrey, TimberWarm and TimberDark; 2.25 m to a texture (_BaseColorMap tiling 1/2.25, kept by PolarHabitatAuthor.WriteMaterials), at 1024 px as PolarHabitatImport holds the pack's textures.
- Validation：Package 0.6.119-test, build ht-01: gameplay 390 and performance 79 pass (Logs/PolarV7/0.6.119-test/ht-01). Before m6-after4 (0.6.118-test), after timber-after, sections b4-intact-ice and b6-habitat in the same order: grain on every timber surface, means within 4% except the unlit heater by day.
- Decisions and scope：weathered_brown_planks (CC0) was set aside for its paint traces and nails; 2048 px timber was left out (about 38 MB more for sharper close views).
- Remaining work：None for the graphics core plan's four milestones.

## Graphics presets and the Graphics tab (M1a)
<!-- project-update-log:2026-11-06:graphics-presets -->

- Reason：The user asked for graphics settings like Forza Horizon 6's: presets plus single options, with today's look as High.
- Implementation：
  - PolarGraphicsProfile (pure): presets Low, Medium, High, Ultra, Extreme and Custom over twelve options; PolarGraphics applies them and raises Changed; settings files version 2, version 1 read as High.
  - Owners read PolarGraphics.Current: HDRP volume tiers, cloud steps, sun and lamp shadow sizes, motion blur, snow relief reach, snow particle rate and cap, sea tessellation, mip limit and anisotropic filtering.
  - A Graphics tab between Picture and Sound with descriptions and costs in its hint line; Footprints moved there; the Display tab's quality stepper removed; maxDirectionalShadowMapResolution 4096.
- Validation：
  - Tools/PolarSettingsCheck 87 checks, including a copy of the player's own version 1 file.
  - 143 art captures at High against a genuine before: median 0.10% of pixels changed, as two same-code after runs (0.08%); b6 compared alone on both sides.
  - Front page 139, startup 4, screen-args 3; gameplay 390 at High and at Low (same world hash); performance 79 at High (paced views equal ht-01; uncapped pond and sea +0.05-0.73 ms GPU over an unpaired ht-01 control, which itself drifted +0.37-0.84 ms).
  - Contact sheets at all five presets (presets-sheet-a.jpg, presets-sheet-b.jpg): thin-ice cracks, open water and the lit window read at Low.
- Decisions and scope：
  - Unity's quality level stays the top one, which Players and captures always used; the five levels stay in QualitySettings.asset, unused.
  - High equals the old constants by construction (HDRP's default Medium tiers, scales of 1).
- Remaining work：
  - M1b live readout and video memory meter, M1c anti-aliasing and upscaling and flag motion vectors, M1d benchmark and paired per-preset performance.
  - Low's day interiors are 25% brighter (SSGI off falls back to the sky probe); left as the Low look.

## Performance overlay and video memory meter (graphics core M1b, 0.6.121-test)
<!-- project-update-log:2026-11-06:performance-overlay -->

- Reason：M1b of the user's Forza Horizon 6 settings plan: a live performance readout and a video memory meter.
- Implementation：
  - PolarPerformanceOverlay: Display's Performance overlay (Off, Frame rate, Full) in the UI's top right corner with no input; Full shows frame time, CPU main and render threads and GPU from FrameTimingManager, the limit, video memory of its budget (DXGI) and the game's memory, rebuilt twice a second.
  - The Graphics tab's video memory meter beside Restore defaults, read once a second, amber past 90%; settings field performanceOverlay (Off by default, file still version 2).
  - PolarSettingsCapture also captures the Graphics tab and the front page with any overlay at Full (capture-only).
- Validation：
  - Tools/PolarSettingsCheck 91; front page 148, startup 4, screen-args 3 on po-03; gameplay 390 and ui 463 on po-01, which differs only inside the overlay.
  - Captures: the meter row, the Display tab's new row and the overlay corner change; the Picture tab is unchanged (control, 0%).
- Decisions and scope：
  - The meter shows the measured reading, not an estimate from per-level deltas: every option applies at once, so the reading follows each change.
  - The limit reads vsync or cap while frames keep within 10% of its interval, since under vertical sync the GPU time counts its wait.
- Remaining work：
  - M1c anti-aliasing and upscaling and flag motion vectors; M1d benchmark and paired per-preset performance.
  - In the Editor, whose Game view does not wait for vertical sync, the limit can read vsync above the refresh rate.

## Anti-aliasing and upscaling choices, flag motion vectors (0.6.122-test)
<!-- project-update-log:2026-11-06:anti-aliasing-upscaling -->

- Reason：M1c of the graphics core plan's M1: Forza Horizon 6's anti-aliasing and upscaling choices, and route flags that TAA and motion blur follow.
- Implementation：Graphics tab rows Anti-aliasing (TAA default, SMAA, TAA upscale, FSR 1, DLSS, FSR 2 where HDRP finds them), Render scale (67/58/50% on software dynamic resolution) and Sharpness; DLSS and FSR 2 from Unity's built-in NVIDIA and AMD modules; route-flag cloth motion in uv5 with RouteFlagCloth.mat (_AddPrecomputedVelocity); the hands and handbook under an upscaler take HDRP's off-screen rescale for light lookups, since HDRP's light lists are at the render size. - Decisions and scope：STP, a free 50-100% scale, dynamic resolution Hold the frame limit and frame generation left out on DX11; a preset never changes the method, scale or sharpness.
